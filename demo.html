AIML-03 · Recommendation demo

Movie recommendations

Explore an offline MovieLens recommender with item-to-item suggestions from liked ratings, explicit genre matching for new users, and a popularity fallback.

What this demo does: without genre preferences, entered movie IDs are treated as liked or highly rated history for item-to-item recommendations. Seen IDs are excluded. Genre preferences use transparent movie metadata matching. This offline demo does not use live user behavior or predict clicks.

Experimental two-stage recommender

Explore a saved MovieLens user through FAISS retrieval and a feature-based ranker. Results are experimental; the models were evaluated on reused development data.
